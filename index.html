Explore Busan

Busan (부산) is South Korea's vibrant coastal capital. A magnificent blend of beautiful beaches, majestic mountains, and futuristic skyscrapers.

📊 Key Facts

  • The 6th busiest container port globally.
  • Hosts the famous Busan International Film Festival (BIFF).
  • Famous for "Satoori" (the energetic local dialect).

🍜 Local Delicacies

  • Dwaeji Gukbap (Hearty Pork Soup)
  • Ssiat Hoddeok (Sweet Seed Pancakes)
  • Busan Eomuk (Famous Fish Cakes)

📸 Famous Landmarks

  • Haeundae Beach & Luxury Skyscrapers
  • Gamcheon Culture Village (Machu Picchu of Busan)
  • Haedong Yonggungsa Ocean Temple
  • Jagalchi Seafood Market
